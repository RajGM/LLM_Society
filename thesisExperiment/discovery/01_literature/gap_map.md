## Priority contributions (thesis “must show” list)

1. **Remapping table:** Pfeffer’s seven → thesis six knobs (honest about cross-media gap).  
2. **Domain transfer:** climate/geoengineering seeds grounded in Debnath events (SCoPEx; chemtrails–Gates narrative window).  
3. **Topology × BP factorial** with \(k^*\) definition on *network-mean* MI (not only linear-branch MPR).  
4. **Correction resistance** after \(n\) exposures (theory: Lewandowsky; instrument: CIKM auditor).  
5. **Clear PRIOR boundary:** CIKM supplies method; thesis supplies climate-firestorm science question.
