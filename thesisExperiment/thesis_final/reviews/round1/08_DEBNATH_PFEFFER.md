# Round 1 expert audit: Debnath graph and Pfeffer application

## Verdict

**Major revision required.** The chapter correctly states the central limitations: the object is hashtag-based rather than a retweet cascade, Debnath supplies no empirical Twitter MPR, Pfeffer et al. list seven original Outlook factors, and the thesis vocabulary is a remapping. However, the provenance is still described too strongly as “empirical,” the 56/63 mapping needs a sharper denominator, and the reported conspiracy-cut modularity is not valid modularity.

## Findings requiring correction

### 1. Critical: the reported modularity values are invalid

`scripts/pfeffer_debnath_vs_sim.js` does not implement standard directed or undirected modularity. Its `modularityConspiracy()` function sums only over observed arcs and divides by `2m`; it does not evaluate the full null-model term over node pairs. The decisive diagnostic is the homogeneous result: the thesis reports \(Q=0.457\) when every node has the same label. Any standard modularity for an all-one-community partition is \(Q=0\).

Independent calculations give:

- undirected modularity on 171 unique pairs, conspiracy versus non-conspiracy: \(Q=0.42967\);
- directed modularity on the 228 arcs: \(Q=0.39412\);
- homogeneous all-conspiracy partition: \(Q=0\) under either definition.

Remove \(Q=0.404\) and \(Q=0.457\), and all interpretations based on them, until one graph convention and a standard implementation are selected and documented. In particular, homogeneous relabelling cannot increase legitimate all-one-community modularity.

### 2. Major: this is a designed reconstruction, not an empirical graph

The 63-node object is a literature-informed, hand-encoded hashtag topology in FakeNewsNet/importer shape. Its nodes are synthetic seats keyed to hashtags, not observed Twitter accounts. Its edges combine selected co-occurrence and same-cluster links; the OSF 5,000-record prefix only annotates some links and explicitly does not change topology. Two expert seats were added for the simulation design.

Therefore, “empirical graph,” “empirical topology,” and “empirical identity” overstate provenance. Use “reconstructed hashtag stand-in,” “literature-informed topology,” and “assigned discourse label.” The current retweet disclaimer is necessary but does not by itself cure the empirical-provenance overclaim.

Primary evidence: `data/derived/debnath_reconstruct_report.md`, `scripts/reconstruct_debnath.js`, and `data/derived/debnath_hashtag_cascade.json`.

### 3. The 63/228 count is correct, but direction has no empirical meaning

Direct inspection verifies 63 nodes and 228 stored arcs, collapsing to 171 unique unordered pairs. Of those pairs, 57 are represented in both directions and 114 in one direction, yielding \(57\times2+114=228\) arcs. These directions are importer/design encoding, not observed retweet, reply, mention, follower, or temporal directions.

The defensible formulation is: “63 synthetic hashtag seats and 228 stored arcs (171 unique unordered pairs).” Do not call the arcs empirically directed edges.

### 4. The 56/63 persona match is internally reproducible but not empirical validation

The count is correct when comparing:

1. `user_profiles[].intended_debnath_bp`, generated by the reconstruction rules; with
2. the mixed D-net config’s `personaId`.

It is therefore a match between two project artefacts, not a match to 63 personas observed or inferred by Debnath et al. No HDBSCAN persona recovery or hydrated-user classification occurred.

The seven exact-ID mismatches are correctly enumerated. Six preserve both the assigned discourse block and intended BP family. The mitigation seat needs special wording: its graph cluster/identity is environmental, while both intended and mixed persona families are climate-action. Thus “all seven preserve discourse type” is true only if “type” means the project’s intended BP family; it is false if it means the graph’s assigned cluster identity.

State the denominator explicitly: “56/63 mixed seats exactly match the reconstruction’s intended persona IDs; all seven remaining seats preserve intended BP family, although `user_mitigation_amp` crosses the graph’s environmental-to-climate-action label boundary.”

### 5. No empirical Twitter MPR: verified

This limitation is correctly stated. Debnath et al. report tweet volume, emotions, toxicity, semantic associations, and hashtag-network measures, not the thesis’s 0–5 MI/MPR instrument. The source IDs were not hydrated into a tweet-level comparison, and the stand-in has no usable empirical clock.

Keep simulated auditor MI, Debnath toxicity (mean 0.17; severe toxicity 0.12), and the project’s hashtag prior strictly separate. None is an empirical Twitter MPR, and they are not interchangeable scales.

### 6. Pfeffer’s seven factors versus the thesis remapping: verified

The original Outlook list is correctly given as:

1. speed and volume of communication;
2. binary choices;
3. network clusters;
4. unrestrained information flow;
5. lack of diversity;
6. cross-media dynamics; and
7. network-triggered decision processes.

Valence and surprise are not numbered Pfeffer factors. The thesis rows—valence, surprise, identity, clustering, echo, temporal, and held cross-media—are an operational remapping. The engine’s `forward`/`reinterpret`/`drop` actions are ternary, so factor 2 is not implemented. The current application states this distinction correctly.

Avoid “seven-factor test” or “seven-factor confirmation”: shock/surprise is held, binary choice is absent, weak-tie flow is not represented at platform scale, the adoption stages are not logged, and cross-media is absent.

## Claim-by-claim audit

### Clustering

The topology-derived values—global transitivity 0.3146, mean local clustering 0.5153, mean unique degree 5.4286, and chemtrails-hub unique degree 33—are reproducible after collapsing arcs to unique undirected adjacency. They describe closure designed into the reconstructed graph before simulation. They are not Debnath’s published country-network estimates, retweet closure, or emergent D-net clustering.

The modularity claims fail for the reason above and must be removed or recomputed.

### Echo

The reported 0.8728 “identity homophily” and 0.8377 family homophily are shares over 228 stored arcs, so reciprocal pairs receive double weight. On 171 unique unordered pairs, the corresponding values are 0.9064 and 0.8596. Neither convention is inherently mandatory, but the denominator must be named and used consistently with the graph convention.

Mixed D-net’s approximately 0.838 block homophily is mechanically inherited from the fixed topology and assigned families; it is not a post-simulation echo outcome. Homogeneous homophily of 1.0 is tautological and provides no evidence of echo formation. The chapter correctly notes the latter point.

### Identity

The 28/19/14/2 graph-label composition and the 28/19/13/3 mixed-persona-family composition are internally reproducible. They are not the same composition: the expert overlay and mitigation mapping shift one environmental seat into the expert/climate-action accounting. Do not say the mixed assignment simply “retains the empirical type-level composition.”

The H-versus-mixed contrast changes both diversity and conspiracy prevalence. It supports a controlled comparison of two persona assignments on one topology, not an isolated causal effect of diversity or identity.

### Temporal

All four primary runs contain event histories for 61 nodes; the climate-scientist and science-journalist seats have none. Those two seats form a separate expert component, so the seed’s connected component contains exactly 61 nodes. Reaching 61/63 is therefore saturation of the seeded component under the engine, not evidence of empirical speed or unusually broad diffusion.

Eight ticks/hops remain simulation units and cannot be converted to minutes, hours, Debnath’s linguistic context window, or Pfeffer’s adoption stages. The absence of an empirical tweet clock is correctly acknowledged.

### Cross-media

The held/absent conclusion is correct. There is no legacy-media, newsroom, broadcast, or exogenous-volume layer. A hierarchical topology is not a substitute for Pfeffer’s social-to-legacy-to-social feedback mechanism.

### Valence and firestorm language

Continuous auditor MI is higher for the chemtrails/Gates article than for SCoPEx in both persona assignments, but the dual instrument reverses that article ordering. This is instrument- and content-specific simulated degradation, not indignation, toxicity, Twitter misinformation, or validation of a firestorm. The current caution is appropriate; retain it.

## Publication-safe bottom line

The defensible contribution is a simulation on a 63-seat, literature-informed hashtag topology. It shows how fixed, pre-wired structure and alternative persona assignments condition simulated auditor outputs. It does not reconstruct user-to-user Twitter diffusion, validate empirical MPR, demonstrate emergent echo chambers, estimate empirical temporal acceleration, or test all seven Pfeffer mechanisms.

Fix the modularity implementation and replace “empirical graph” language before acceptance.
