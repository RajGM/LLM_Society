
\chapter{Conclusion}
\label{chap:conclusion}

This thesis examined how persona composition and network conditions affect
outputs from two factual-degradation instruments and interpreted the design
using Pfeffer's firestorm framework. The evidence supports a bounded
measurement-sensitivity study. It does not support a model of real Twitter
users, and it does not test whether an online firestorm occurred.

\section{Answers to the research questions}

\paragraph{RQ1: measurement.}
The continuous and dual-discrete auditors produced different levels and weakly
aligned network-condition rankings. Dual-discrete live-cell mean MI exceeded
continuous live-cell mean MI on all eight network-condition aggregates in both
identity arms. The mean topology gaps were 0.8201 for H and 1.0450 for He.
These values show whole-pipeline sensitivity, including different missing-item
penalties. They do not show that one auditor measured the true amount of
misinformation. Continuous and discrete scores must therefore remain separate.

\paragraph{RQ2: persona composition.}
Named composition described the results more consistently than the pooled H/He
label. Homogeneous conspiracy-family means were 2.2194 continuous and 3.1691
dual-discrete. Science and environment means were 0.1118 and 0.8687. The
conspiracy-heavy \texttt{mix\_00} also exceeded conspiracy-free
\texttt{mix\_02} under both instruments. These contrasts apply to the executed
prompts and model. They do not establish a general effect of human diversity.

\paragraph{RQ3: network conditions and survival.}
Bundled network conditions altered same-regime H/He contrasts, live-cell mean
MI, and scorable-cell survival, but no universal ranking emerged. Continuous
He--H differences changed sign across the eight graphs. Dual-discrete
differences were positive on all eight. Dead-cell rates also varied by
condition, and live-only means describe surviving cascades. The observed
\(k^{*}\) values are eight-tick, instrument-specific outcomes. They are not
empirical ignition times.

\paragraph{RQ4: constructed-graph stress test.}
The 63-node D-net used four configurations and eight article-level cells on a
constructed hashtag graph. Conspiracy-only H exceeded the mixed assignment for
both articles under both instruments. This recurrence is not a replication.
Structural correspondence remained partial. Breadth met the selected
tolerance, whereas depth and structural virality did not. The Digital Twin
Fidelity Score was 0.2754 and the implemented check failed. Homogeneous
one-community modularity is \(Q=0\). D-net is therefore a documented stress
test, not a validated retweet cascade or a source of empirical Twitter MPR.

\section{Contributions and boundary}

The thesis contributes a theory-to-design ledger, a 1,728-cell climate
factorial, a measurement-sensitivity analysis, a composition-conditioned
interpretation, and a documented 63-node stress test. The canonical harvest
contains 288 configurations, 1,728 cells, and 292 hatched-dead cells. It uses
\texttt{gpt-4o-mini}, eight processing ticks, and one retained run per
configuration. The C5 ``combined'' result is a table union. It is not a
physical supergraph. Its execution is traceable but not exactly reproducible:
the selected metadata record 64,270 HTTP-error responses, failed rewrites can
propagate unchanged text, and failed audits remain unscored. No auditor
parse/all-correct fallback was observed. Restricting the analysis to
zero-error runs reverses the small continuous He--H contrast (\(-0.0295\)
rather than \(+0.1549\)); the dual-discrete contrast remains positive.
Accordingly, the continuous collapsed-arm direction is not a robust headline
conclusion. No human factuality, realism, or persona-validity evaluation was
conducted.

Pfeffer, Zorbach, and Carley identified seven Outlook factors
\parencite{pfeffer2014firestorms}. The six-knob vocabulary in this thesis is a
computational remapping. Cross-media dynamics remained held. Empirical timing
was unavailable. The engine's ternary actions did not implement the original
binary-choice factor. These omissions prevent a seven-factor confirmation.

\section{Outlook}

The next study should prioritise a smaller, preregistered validation design
over a larger unreplicated grid. Independent graph and model seeds should
support uncertainty estimates. Matched persona compositions should isolate
diversity from prevalence. Independent human coders should assess factuality
and realism under an approved protocol. Longer horizons should test censoring
at tick eight. Lawfully obtained timestamped interaction data would permit a
genuine temporal and cascade comparison. Until independent replication, human
validation, longer-horizon analysis, and empirical temporal data are
available, the testbed should be used only for mechanism-oriented simulation
and measurement-sensitivity analysis. This revision does not assign or
guarantee a grade.
