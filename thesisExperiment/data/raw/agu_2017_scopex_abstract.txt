Keutsch, F. N. / Keith / Dykema
Stratospheric Controlled Perturbation Experiment (SCoPEx) is a scientific experiment to advance understanding of stratospheric aerosols. It aims to make quantitative measurements of aerosol microphysics and atmospheric chemistry to improve large-scale models used to assess the risks and benefits of solar geoengineering. A propelled balloon gondola forms a well-mixed volume (roughly 1 km long and 100 meters in diameter) into which aerosols (e.g., < 1 kg of 0.3 µm radius CaCO3 particles) can be injected.
